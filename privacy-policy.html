3. Data Collection When Visiting This Website

When you access my website, the hosting provider automatically collects and stores information in server log files. These may include:

  • IP address (anonymized or temporarily stored)

  • Browser type and version

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest)

The log data is deleted automatically after a short retention period unless needed for security or legal reasons.


4. Local Hosting of Fonts

All fonts used on this website are hosted locally on my own server. No connection is made to third-party providers such as Google, and no personal data is transmitted to external servers when fonts are loaded. No consent is required for this.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a functional, visually consistent website)


5. Contact by Email

If you contact me by email, I process the information you provide to respond to your inquiry. This may include your email address, your message, and any other details you choose to share.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your inquiry relates to a contract or pre-contractual measures; otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in responding to inquiries).


6. Cookies and Tracking

This website:

Only technically necessary server-side processes occur. No consent banner is required.

  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)

To exercise any rights, contact me at the email above.
