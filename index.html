About Me

I successfully completed a 2-year apprenticeship as a 3D developer in Heidelberg, Germany and additionally gained experience in the industry through my three months long internship at elaspix. I love tackling complex problems and finding solutions that push the team ahead. That curiosity extends across the whole 3D pipeline.
